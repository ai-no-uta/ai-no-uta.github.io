Lots of staring at the clock run away until I had no choice but to surrender what little I had written up.

When I was taking introductory classes, I thought I liked analysis more than algebra. I think this is not true now, although I have found this out unfortunately recently. I might still write some things on analysis, though, if something piques my interest.
