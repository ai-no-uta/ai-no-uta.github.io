Chinese Culture

Usage in the modern day PRC

Many of the current values for traditional Chinese units of measure were set in the following ROC directive of 1929: 度量衡法.

In 1959, the PRC issued the following directive : 国务院关于統一我国計量制度的命令 proclaiming the metric system to be the basic system of units used in the country while simultaneously continuing the authorization of the use of traditional units, whose relation to the metric system was previously specified under the ROC government.

This directive also fixes the 兩 to be 1/10th of a 斤, rather than the previous 1/16th. This directive does not address whether units minor to the 兩 are adjusted correspondingly, so I have omitted them from my tables below. If someone can speak to contemporary usage, please tell me!

The following proposed (?? its legal effect is not clear to me) directive in 1981 retains only certain measures of length, area, and mass and states their conversions with metric units: 中华人民共和国计量单位名称与符号方案（试行）

It seems that traditional measures were officially to be deprecated by the end of 1990, according to the 国务院关于在我国统一实行法定计量单位的命令 article 2: The market units currently in use in our country's people's daily lives may be permitted in usage until 1990. Before the end of 1990, the shift to the nationally legally promulgated units of measure [SI units] should be completed. The conversion of agricultural units of area should be implemented on the basis of research, for which other arrangements shall be made.

This is out of step with popular usage. Many traditional units are still commonly used, I think; the most commonly used is by far the 斤, but I trust that the average Chinese person is familiar with at least all of the measures permitted in the 1981 directive.
